RentPakistan was built to make finding a place to rent or list a property simple, transparent and fast - whether you're searching for a 5 Marla house in Lahore, a serviced flat in Islamabad, or a shop on a busy Peshawar road.

We cover houses, apartments, flats, rooms, hostels, hotels, guest houses, villas, farm houses and commercial spaces across every major city and dozens of housing societies nationwide.
